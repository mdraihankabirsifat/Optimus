The critical requirement is that exchanging the last heart must grant the Move and must NOT trigger immediate ordinary zero-health elimination.

STATE MODEL:
- Active: positive health, ordinary cave rules.
- Last-heart grace: zero health caused specifically by a successful heart exchange, still playable, fixed 20-second deadline.
- Eliminated: ordinary terminal cave elimination/spectating.
- Qualified/duel: existing safe qualification and separate duel rules.

LAST-HEART GRACE:
- Begin exactly once when exchange consumes the final health.
- Allow movement, jumping, gravity use, boxes and reaching the destination during the 20 seconds.
- Show an unmistakable “Last heart spent — 20s remaining” countdown, distinct from Rush time. Display both when applicable.
- At deadline, eliminate once and enter the existing spectator/results flow.

- Default literal interpretation: a Heart Refill during grace may restore health, but does not cancel the promised 20-second death deadline. Clearly explain “last-heart countdown cannot be cancelled” before this final exchange. Do not invent a hidden rescue rule.

- Ordinary zero health from hazards/damage outside this exchange state still eliminates immediately after applicable existing protections.
- Heart sacrifice is a resource transaction, not a hazard hit: shields, invulnerability and Second Chance must not make it free or produce duplicate rewards.
- Qualifying before the deadline exits the cave state, clears its deadline, and preserves Prompt-2 safe waiting/fresh duel health. No delayed cave callback may kill a finalist in the duel.
- Elimination, leaving the match, replacement/disconnect handling and rematch must clean up or correctly preserve the authoritative deadline. Reconnection cannot restart it.
- The cave exchange system is unavailable in the Freedom Duel.

- Validate eligibility, deduct health, grant exactly one Move and begin grace atomically on authority.
- Reject requests during loading/countdown/transition locks as appropriate, after qualification/elimination, at zero health, with regeneration enabled, or at the charge cap.
- A queued request must revalidate current state before committing.
- Use debouncing/request identity consistent with current networking to avoid duplicate exchange from repeated RPCs.
- Snapshot health, Moves, grace state and authoritative deadline together.
- At competing finish/death deadlines, use a documented deterministic ordering. Recommended: finish at or before the grace deadline qualifies if no earlier authoritative elimination occurred.
- An earlier Rush deadline still resolves the cave; grace never extends Rush time.
- Offline true pause pauses cave simulation and grace together; online menus pause neither.

Acceptance:

[ ] Ordinary lethal damage still works.
[ ] Qualification clears cave-only death callbacks.
